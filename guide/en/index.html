The main screen

See how much is left for the month and where your money is.

  1. Open Zhyto — you land on “Overview”.
  2. The ring shows how much you can still spend this month.
  3. Below are this month’s income and spending, and money on your accounts.
  4. The arrows at the top switch months.

Record a purchase

The sooner you record it, the more accurate your budget. Pick whatever is easiest.

  1. Tap the big “+” at the bottom.
  2. Choose how: “Receipt” — photograph the receipt, “Voice” — say “coffee 4”, “Bank” — a screenshot from your bank, “Manual” — amount and category.
  3. Check the amount, category and account.
  4. Tap “Save”.

Income and salary

See what came in — and don’t enter your salary every month.

  1. Tap “+”, then “Manual”.
  2. At the top switch “Expense” to “Income”.
  3. Enter the amount and tap “Comes every month” — Zhyto will remind you every month.
  4. Tap “Save”.

Accounts and cards

Each card and your cash is its own account, so you see where the money is.

  1. Open “Settings” → “Accounts”.
  2. Tap “+” to add a card, cash or a credit card.
  3. When recording, pick the account you paid from. In a family, first pick the person — “Me” or a name — to see only their accounts.
  4. “Order” at the top lets you arrange them.

monobank adds spending by itself

Connect once and payments from your mono cards appear automatically. A Pro feature, free for the first 14 days.

  1. Open “Settings” → “Banks”.
  2. Follow the link and get your personal read-only token in monobank.
  3. Paste it into Zhyto and pick your cards.
  4. Done: new payments arrive by themselves. The token stays only on your phone.

Apple Pay on iPhone

Tap your iPhone at the till — the purchase is already in Zhyto. A one-minute, one-time setup. A Pro feature.

  1. Open “Settings” → “Apple Pay auto-recording”.
  2. Follow the steps on screen and swipe the pictures below them: Shortcuts → Automation → “+” → Wallet.
  3. The first payment with a new card: Zhyto asks which account it belongs to.
  4. Changed your mind? On the same screen tap the card under “Cards” and pick another account — its earlier entries move along.

Bank notifications on Android

A notification from your bank arrives — and the purchase is already in Zhyto. A Pro feature.

  1. Open “Settings” → “Auto-recording”.
  2. Tap the button and allow Zhyto to read notifications.
  3. When a new bank sends a notification, Zhyto asks which account it goes to.
  4. If something was recorded wrong, fix the entry — Zhyto remembers.

Family: a budget together

Everyone records their own spending; shared items are visible to both. Personal ones can be marked “only me”.

  1. Open “Settings” → “Family” and create a family.
  2. Tap “Create code and share” and send the code to your partner — they enter it in their Zhyto.
  3. Sent each other money? Record a “Transfer” — Zhyto won’t count it twice.
  4. Who owes whom is shown in “Family”.

Plans: regular payments and goals

Never forget a bill — and see how close you are to a dream.

  1. Open the “Plans” tab.
  2. Regular payments: “Add” — name, amount, day. Zhyto reminds you.
  3. Goals: “Add” — what you’re saving for and how much.
  4. Zhyto spots subscriptions charged every month and offers to add them.

Where the money goes

See what you spend most on — the conclusions are yours.

  1. Open the “Insights” tab.
  2. Switch between spending, income and balance at the top.
  3. Tap a category to see all its entries.

Pro and 14 days free

Pro adds unlimited AI, monobank, Apple Pay and Family. The first 14 days are free.

  1. Open “Settings” and tap the Pro card.
  2. Pick yearly or monthly and start the free trial.
  3. Cancel any time in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions — at least a day before the trial ends and you won’t be charged.

Settings and help

Language, currency, lock and contacting us — all in one place.

  1. Open the “Settings” tab.
  2. Here are the language, main currency and Face ID or fingerprint lock.
  3. This guide is always here: “How to use Zhyto”.
  4. Questions — “Support”, we answer by email.
